LOCAL-FIRST BY DESIGN

Your library. Your browser.

PrivateFlix is designed to know as little about your private collection as possible.

Local library, remote posters

PrivateFlix never scans, clears or uploads your browsing history. It reads the active page after a direct save action, and reads saved pages only when you explicitly run Refresh all media.
